# Reaction to Opinion (Реакция на чужое мнение)

A psychological text-based horror/detective game about surviving toxic environments, protecting your boundaries, and finding your true self.

## 🧠 About the Game
You play as a girl surrounded by people who try to break you, copy you, and gaslight you. Your task is not just to survive, but to keep your identity intact. 

The game is based on real psychological concepts: CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy), Spiral Dynamics, neurobiology of stress, and trauma responses. Every choice you make is a reflection of your inner boundaries.

## ⚙️ Core Mechanics
*   **Ego Bar (Целостность Я):** Your mental health. NPCs attack it with manipulations, passive aggression, and gaslighting.
*   **Defense Cards:** Reflect attacks using boundaries, CBT techniques, and self-reflection.
*   **The Shadow (Тень):** If your Ego drops too low, the screen gets covered in a dirty texture, and you must pass a self-identity test to return to reality.
*   **Yellow Level:** The ultimate goal is to reach the "Yellow Level" of awareness (Spiral Dynamics), where you see reality clearly and don't engage in toxic games.

## 🎮 How to Play
1. Clone or download the repository.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
3. No installation or server required. Just pure HTML, CSS, and JS.
